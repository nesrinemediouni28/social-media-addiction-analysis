# Social Media Addiction Among Students

## Project Overview

This project analyzes survey data on social media usage and addiction among students.
The objective was to explore patterns between daily social media usage, addiction scores, sleep, academic performance, mental well-being, and social media-related conflicts.
The analysis was completed using **Microsoft Power BI**, including data cleaning, transformation, DAX calculations, data visualization, and dashboard design.

> **Note:** This project was completed as an independent learning project. The original Power BI (.pbix) file is no longer available, so this repository documents the analysis, dashboard, and findings from the completed project.

---

## Objectives

The analysis focused on three main questions:

1. How does daily social media usage relate to addiction scores?
2. How are addiction levels related to sleep and mental health indicators?
3. Do students report academic or social effects associated with their social media usage?

## Key Findings

### Addiction and Usage

Students reporting higher daily social media usage tended to have higher addiction scores.

### Sleep

Higher addiction categories were associated with lower reported sleep hours.

### Academic Impact

**64.26%** of respondents reported that social media affects their academic performance.

### Platforms

Instagram and TikTok were among the most commonly used platforms, particularly among students in higher addiction categories.

### Mental Health

Average reported mental health scores decreased across higher addiction categories in the analyzed dataset.

### Social Media Conflicts

Students reporting conflicts related to social media tended to have higher addiction scores than students reporting no conflicts.
